Nikolai's HCI Design Rationale

Prepared for live checking with my instructor. Every section on this page applies empirical Human-Computer Interaction laws to earn voluntary engagement far beyond 45 seconds:

Q1: How did you keep the user engaged for 45+ seconds without trapping them?

Instead of passive walls of text or forced waiting screens, the portfolio is architected as 4 interactive micro-simulations plus a real-time UX Thermal Heatmap. Following the Doherty Threshold (<400ms system response), every click yields immediate visual, mathematical, and acoustic feedback.

Q2: How are Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics demonstrated?
  • #1 Visibility of System Status: Live 45s ring timer, Usability Score gauge (`22→100`), live 911 ETA telemetry, and RJ45 LEDs.
  • #3 User Control & Freedom: Instant `ESC` modal exits, 1-click sandbox resets, and non-blocking navigation.
  • #5 Error Prevention: Proven live in Lab 01 by repairing deceptive destructive buttons and adding a 2-step confirmation safeguard.
  • #6 Recognition Rather Than Recall: 1-click SQL and Cisco IOS command chips in Lab 03.
Q3: Where are Fitts's Law & Hick's Law applied?

Hick's Law ($RT = a + b \log_2(n)$): Applied in the 4-Tab Hero Matrix and in Lab 01 Toggle #4 (collapsing 8 redundant checkout inputs into 2 streamlined fields). Fitts's Law ($ID = \log_2(2D/W)$): Proven with a live linear regression scatter plot in Lab 04 and target sizing in Lab 01 Toggle #1.
